My Story

I am 28 years old, born and raised in São Paulo, Brazil, where I completed my Bachelor’s degree in Advertising and Communications. From an early age, I felt drawn to communication, visual arts, photography, and the beauty of discovering new places and cultures.

My desire to explore the world and step outside my comfort zone led me to move to the United States as an Au Pair. For two years, I lived with an American family—a deeply transformative experience that became a cornerstone of both my personal maturity and professional perspective.

"Living abroad allowed me to learn a new language, navigate a new culture, and become truly independent. It changed not only how I approach my career, but also what I want for my life and future."

Today, living in New York and continuing my education, I bring this global mindset, adaptability, and personal growth into every creative and strategic project I undertake.
