My Story

I am a 28-year-old communicator and strategist originally from São Paulo, Brazil, where I graduated with my Bachelor's degree in Advertising and Communications. Growing up, I was always captivated by stories, visual arts, and the beauty of discovering new places and cultures.

Driven by that desire to explore, I took a leap that changed my life: I moved to the United States to spend two years as an Au Pair living with an American family. Stepping into a whole new world allowed me to master English, immerse myself in a different culture, build deep relationships, and view the world from an entirely new perspective.

"Living abroad transformed more than just my lifestyle—it shifted how I view my life, my relationships, and what I want to build in my career."

Today, I am based in New York City while pursuing my MBA through a university in Los Angeles. Living at the intersection of creative advertising, business strategy, and global experience has shaped my personal and professional purpose.

Future Vision

I am building toward an exciting entrepreneurial goal: launching my own branding and business consulting agency dedicated specifically to personal trainers and fitness professionals.

Combining my Advertising degree, MBA studies, and passion for the gym lifestyle to help personal trainers develop powerful personal brands, improve their client communication, and build thriving businesses.

Personal trainers are incredible at transforming people's physical lives, but often lack the strategic marketing tools to position themselves in a crowded industry. My mission is to give them the brand clarity and business strategy they need to scale.
