My Story

My journey began in Brazil, where I earned my Bachelor's degree in Advertising. I have always held a deep curiosity for the world—a drive to discover new places, immerse myself in different cultures, and understand what connects people across background and language.

Driven by this passion, I moved to the United States to serve as an Au Pair, spending two transformative years living with an American family. Immersing myself in a new environment, mastering a second language, and navigating everyday life in another culture shifted my perspective profoundly.

"Living abroad wasn't just a change of scenery—it fundamentally evolved how I see life, communication, and my future career."

During my time in the U.S., my passion for photography and storytelling grew alongside a newfound love for fitness and the gym lifestyle. Experiencing firsthand how personal transformation builds confidence sparked a clear vision for where I want to take my career next.

Future Vision

Today, I am combining my academic foundation in Advertising, my active MBA studies, my international background, and my enthusiasm for fitness to achieve a focused entrepreneurial goal:

Building a brand & business consulting agency dedicated to helping personal trainers elevate their voice, build authentic personal brands, and sustainably grow their fitness businesses.

Fitness professionals excel at changing lives, but often lack the strategic marketing tools to position themselves effectively. I aim to bridge that gap with tailored branding and business strategy.
